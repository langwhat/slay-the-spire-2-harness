# Core

Keep the deck's core in the strategy notes and decide every deck change against it.

Links. Read every owned card and relic together with the powers it applies. A link exists when one object produces something specific that another consumes, listens for, or repeats: an event (a card exhausted, discarded, drawn, or played, HP lost, block gained), a generated card, a card returned or replayed, or energy and draws that a named card needs. A bonus that every attack or every block card receives, such as more damage per hit or a debuff on the enemy, is not a link to each of them. It links only to the few cards that multiply it most.

The core is the linked group whose combined turn is strongest:

- growth: output rises with each turn or trigger;
- cycle: draw, energy return, and in-fight exhaust shrink the circulating deck until the same few cards are played every turn, or several times in one turn;
- burst: a few cards that end the fight within one or two turns once assembled.

`core` lists at most five owned cards and relics: the growth source, what triggers it, and what turns the growth into damage. It is not an inventory of the deck: energy, draw, and block cards stay out, and so does any card that works without the others. Record a core as soon as two owned cards or relics are linked, in any act; until then leave it empty.

`plan` states the core's turn in at most 300 characters: its cards in play order, the battle turn by which they are normally drawn and paid for, the damage on that turn and two turns later, and what the core still lacks. Exhausted cards and powers already played no longer occupy draws. Check those numbers against the HP and damage of the act's Elites and Boss. A core that does not kill them in time leaves the deck short of damage, and a core whose cost to its owner grows each turn must win before that cost does. While `core` is empty, `plan` says what the act's Elites and Boss still demand of the deck.

Removal, transformation, and in-fight exhaust of basic and transition cards make the core ready sooner; rank them above adding cards. Upgrade first the card whose upgrade most raises the core's growth per turn or per trigger, then the one that removes its bottleneck.

After each battle report, rewrite `plan` from what limited the fight. Core cards arrived late or were not drawn: fewer cards and more draw, not more damage. The core was ready and the fight still ran long: growth or damage. The fight ended on time and cost HP: block.

Supplied knowledge may list cores known for this character. When owned cards match one, build it. A group that is not listed can still be real when the rules and links support it.
