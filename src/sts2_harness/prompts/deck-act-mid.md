## Stage threshold: middle acts

Hands already hold chosen cards, normal fights hit hard early, and the deck needs coherent growth and mitigation for Elites and a high-HP Act Boss.

- Merely beating a basic starter is seldom enough now: lean strongly toward skipping standalone flat-number cards that trail your best owned card in that role, since each extra transition card delays your core in every fight left.
- Once a core exists, judge every candidate against delaying its setup and payoff turns. Avoid adding extra copies of flat block, conditional utility, or secondary damage just to feel more consistent in a growing deck; prefer skipping, thinning, or genuine draw, fetch, or multipliers that shorten the turn your core wins.
- Favor completing a link with owned cards or relics, multiplying an active core, adding net draw or energy so setup lands earlier, or covering multi-enemy waves and peak Boss hits your defense misses.
- Check average hand cost and `circulating` before taking slow setup or 2+ cost cards without energy or frontload to support them.
