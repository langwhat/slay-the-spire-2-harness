## Stage threshold: middle acts

Hands already hold chosen cards, normal fights hit hard early, and the deck needs coherent growth and mitigation for Elites and a high-HP Act Boss.

- Merely beating a basic starter is seldom enough now: lean toward skipping standalone flat-number cards that trail your best owned card in that role, since each extra transition card delays your core in every fight left.
- Favor completing or multiplying a real core (testing whether its post-setup ceiling can close high-HP Bosses via fast-kill burst or sustained mitigation), adding net draw or energy so setup and opening mitigation land earlier, or covering multi-enemy waves and peak hits your current mitigation and draw miss.
- Check `circulating`, hand cost, and growing downsides before taking slow setup, additional 2+ cost cards, or extra low-impact filler without energy or ceiling upside.
