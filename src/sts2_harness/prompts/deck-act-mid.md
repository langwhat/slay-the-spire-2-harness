## Stage threshold: middle acts

Hands already hold chosen cards, normal fights hit hard early, and the deck needs coherent growth and mitigation for Elites and a high-HP Act Boss.

- Merely beating a basic starter is seldom enough now: lean strongly toward skipping standalone flat-number cards that trail your best owned card in that role, since each extra transition card delays your core in every fight left.
- Once a core exists, test whether its post-setup ceiling actually beats this act's Boss and the final Bosses—either via fast-kill burst or sustained scaling defense—and judge every candidate against delaying its setup and payoff turns. Avoid adding low-impact flat block, conditional utility, or secondary damage just to feel more consistent in a growing deck; prefer skipping, thinning, or genuine draw, fetch, energy, multipliers, or scaling defense that raise your ceiling or shorten setup. Only add a standalone spike mitigation card when `fights` still show heavy opening HP loss and fast draw, fetch, or opening defense relics are lacking.
- Favor completing a link with owned cards or relics, multiplying an active core, adding net draw or energy so setup and opening defense land earlier, or covering multi-enemy waves and peak Boss hits your mitigation misses.
- Check average hand cost and `circulating` before taking slow setup or 2+ cost cards without energy or frontload to support them.
