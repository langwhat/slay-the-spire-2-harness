The report's before and after values are observations at the battle boundaries. Their difference does not identify damage or resource-change causes. `result` is `won` or `lost` when confirmed, otherwise `unknown`. `last_observed_turn` is the last observed turn number.

`previous_battle` retains the latest battle report as historical evidence, scoped by the report's location. It is not a claim about current resources; use it when relevant to the present choice.
