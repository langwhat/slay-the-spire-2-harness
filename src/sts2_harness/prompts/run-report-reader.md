The report's before and after values are observations at the battle boundaries. Their difference does not identify damage or resource-change causes. `last_observed_turn` is the last observed turn number.

`previous_battle` retains the latest battle report and review as historical evidence, scoped by the report's location. It is not a new handoff task or a claim about current resources. Use it when relevant to the present choice; Combat review remains advice, not an observed cause.
