## Stage threshold: first act

Hands begin mostly as basic starting attacks and blocks, and the immediate job is beating upcoming fights, Act 1 Elites on the route ahead, and this act's Boss.

- Strong standalone frontload, multi-enemy reach, and efficient block that clearly beat basic starters are worth adding early so Act 1 Elites pay out relics safely.
- Once three or four added cards—transition answers alongside any early growth—already cover this act's Elites and Boss, tighten sharply and default toward skipping unless a candidate starts or multiplies a real Boss core, adds true net draw or reliable energy, or fills an open role or clearly beats your best owned card in that job—not merely because it shares a minor tag or costs energy to draw 1.
- Take early growth sources, battle-long multipliers, payoffs with already-supported triggers, reliable energy, and true net draw when offered—they appear less often than plain attacks. Once one clear core forms, stop adding unrelated side-engines; do not skip a genuinely empty Act 1 survival or damage role for multiple floors waiting on a rare scaling piece.
- Even in Act 1, lean against stacking multiple 2+ cost cards without energy support, or taking a narrow payoff before an imminent Elite with zero owned triggers.
