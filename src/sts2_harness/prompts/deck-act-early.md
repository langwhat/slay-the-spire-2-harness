## Stage threshold: first act

Hands begin mostly as basic starters, so the immediate job is beating upcoming fights, Act 1 Elites, and the Act 1 Boss without bloating later acts.

- Early strong frontload, multi-enemy reach, and efficient block that clearly beat basic starters are worth adding so Act 1 Elites pay out relics safely.
- Check `deck_stats.added`: once 3–4 added cards already cover this act's Elites and Boss, tighten sharply and default toward skipping through the rest of the act unless a candidate starts or multiplies a real Boss core, adds true net draw or reliable energy, or fills an open survival or multi-target role.
- Take early growth sources, battle-long multipliers, supported payoffs, reliable energy, and true net draw when offered—they appear less often than plain attacks. Once one clear core forms, stop adding unrelated side-engines; do not skip a genuinely empty Act 1 survival or damage role waiting on a rare scaling piece.
- Even in Act 1, lean against stacking multiple 2+ cost cards without energy support, or taking a narrow payoff before an imminent Elite with zero owned triggers.
