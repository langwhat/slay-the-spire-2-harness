## Stage threshold: first act

Hands begin mostly as basic starting attacks and blocks, and the immediate job is beating upcoming fights, Act 1 Elites on the route ahead, and this act's Boss.

- Strong standalone frontload, multi-enemy reach, and efficient block that clearly beat basic starters are worth adding early so Act 1 Elites pay out relics safely.
- Once two or three added transition cards—alongside any early growth—already cover this act's Elites and Boss, lean toward skipping further standalone flat-number cards that do not start a link, add draw or energy, or beat your best owned card in that job.
- Take early growth sources, payoffs with common partners, reliable energy, and net draw when offered—they appear less often than plain attacks, and holding two plausible starts early is normal.
- Even in Act 1, lean against stacking 2+ cost cards past `base_energy` without energy support, or taking a narrow payoff before an imminent Elite with zero owned triggers.
