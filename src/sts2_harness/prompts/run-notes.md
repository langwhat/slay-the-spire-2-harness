# Strategy notes

Keep the deck's core in the strategy notes, which Combat reads at the start of each battle.

`core` lists at most five owned cards and relics: sources, what triggers them, and payoffs. It is not an inventory of the deck: a card belongs only when the core's turn does not work without it. An energy, draw, or block card belongs when it is such a part, and stays out when another card of its kind would do as well. Record the strongest group only once a real compounding or multiplier link exists—do not fill `core` with starter pairings, flat transition cards, or a lone flat value power just to populate slots; until a real core exists, leave it empty. Add a card taken to start a stronger group, and rewrite `core` when that group becomes the stronger one.

`plan` stays within 300 characters. When `core` is non-empty, state how it beats the next Boss—the cards and relics that deal its damage, the turn they are normally in play, the damage per turn from then on against that Boss's HP when known—and only the single sharpest bottleneck still short (early damage, damage to several enemies, repeatable block for incoming hits, or growth), not a wishlist. While `core` is empty, concisely state how the deck currently handles upcoming Elites and this act's Boss plus any uncovered basic survival or Boss demands (such as zero repeatable block) until basic coverage is in place; once covered, narrow to the single sharpest bottleneck. Exhausted cards and powers already played no longer occupy draws. A core whose cost to its owner grows each turn must win before that cost does. When `plan` advises which enemy to attack first, give its condition: how many actions focusing the main enemy versus first removing the damage source would take, the turn it should work, and the attacks taken until then, so Combat can switch when progress falls behind.

`other` holds the pairing for a potion whose value depends on the card it acts on, such as one that repeats or multiplies the next card (POTION.DUPLICATOR, POTION.GIGANTIFICATION_POTION): the owned card it should go with, the fight and turn where that pairing pays most, and the fallback when that card is not drawn in time. Write it when such a potion is obtained, revise it when the deck or the next Boss changes, and keep it concise (aim for at most 500 characters).

After each battle report, rewrite `plan` from what limited the fight. Core cards arrived late or were not drawn: fewer cards and more draw. The core was ready and the fight still ran long: growth or damage. The fight ended on time and cost HP: block. A fight that cost far more HP than others of its kind, or a large share of max HP, stays in `plan`, with its cause, until the deck answers it. When ready to act or travel, include the updated note fields in run_act or run_next so the notes are written before that action. Use run_notes for reading and for writing notes during handoff, when no action accompanies the update.
