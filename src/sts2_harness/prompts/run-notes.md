# Strategy notes

Keep the deck's core in the strategy notes, which Combat reads at the start of each battle.

`core` lists at most five owned cards and relics: sources, what triggers them, and payoffs. It is not an inventory of the deck: a card belongs only when the core's turn does not work without it. An energy, draw, or block card belongs when it is such a part, and stays out when another card of its kind would do as well. Record the strongest group as soon as two owned cards or relics are linked, in any act; until then leave it empty. Add a card taken to start a stronger group, and rewrite `core` when that group becomes the stronger one.

`plan` states in at most 300 characters how the deck will beat the next Boss: the cards and relics that deal its damage, the battle turn by which they are normally in play, the damage per turn from then on against that Boss's HP when known, and which demand is still short (early damage, damage to several enemies, block for the largest hits, or growth), the most urgent first. Exhausted cards and powers already played no longer occupy draws. A core whose cost to its owner grows each turn must win before that cost does. While `core` is empty, `plan` says what the deck still needs to beat the act's Elites and Boss.

After each battle report, rewrite `plan` from what limited the fight. Core cards arrived late or were not drawn: fewer cards and more draw. The core was ready and the fight still ran long: growth or damage. The fight ended on time and cost HP: block. A fight that cost far more HP than others of its kind, or a large share of max HP, stays in `plan`, with its cause, until the deck answers it. When ready to act or travel, include the updated note fields in run_act or run_next so the notes are written before that action. Use run_notes for reading and for writing notes during handoff, when no action accompanies the update.
