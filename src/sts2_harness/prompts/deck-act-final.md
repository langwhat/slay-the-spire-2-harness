## Stage threshold: final act

Few fights remain and the run ends against the final Bosses—potentially two in a row without healing between them.

- Default toward skipping: with few fights left, check `first_pass_turns` and `cycle_turns` before adding any card that delays your strongest core and survival turns across back-to-back Bosses.
- Take a card when it clearly beats keeping the current draw pool thinner across the remaining Boss fights: finishing a missing scaling multiplier or sustained defense ceiling, answering a lethal gap your current deck misses, or adding true net draw, unconditional fetch, or cheap energy that enables setup and defense together.
