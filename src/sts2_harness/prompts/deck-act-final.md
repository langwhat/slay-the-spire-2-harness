## Stage threshold: final act

Few fights remain to draw a new card, and the run ends against the final Bosses—potentially two in a row without healing between them.

- Skip by default: every non-essential circulating card stretches `cycle_turns` and compounds dilution—especially when `deck_stats.basic_and_curse_pct` or `deck_stats.basic_cards` is still high—delaying your strongest core and survival cards on critical Boss turns. Marginal transition cards, pre-upgraded flat cards, paid zero-net-draw or conditional utility, redundant defense, low-impact 0-cost cards, and side-engine pieces rarely improve final Boss odds. Spare shop gold is likewise poor grounds for a mediocre card when removing curses or basic cards, buying relics, or stocking Boss potions helps more.
- Take a card only when adding it clearly raises your odds across the final Boss fights over keeping the current draw pool thinner: finishing a missing growth multiplier or sustained defense ceiling, answering a Boss constraint or lethal peak hit your current mitigation and draw miss, or adding true net draw, unconditional fetch, or cheap energy that lets multi-energy setup and defense happen on the same turn.
