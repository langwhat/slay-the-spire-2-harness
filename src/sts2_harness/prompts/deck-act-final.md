## Stage threshold: final act

Few fights remain to draw a new card, and the run ends against the final Bosses—potentially two in a row without healing between them.

- Lean strongly toward skipping by default: every non-essential card lowers the odds of drawing your core and survival answers on critical Boss turns. Pre-upgraded flat cards, redundant copies of already-covered utility or low-impact defense, and low-impact 0-cost cards rarely change the final Boss outcome. Spare shop gold is likewise poor grounds for a mediocre card when removals, upgrades, or potions help more.
- Take a card when it directly changes how the final Boss fights go: finishing a missing growth multiplier or sustained defense ceiling, answering a Boss constraint or peak hit your current mitigation and draw miss, or adding fast draw or cheap energy that lets multi-energy setup and defense happen on the same turn.
